Amazon, through three lenses

Business quality, price, and portfolio fit deserve their own answers.

A great business still needs a sensible price.

This memo brings three independent perspectives into one place. The aim is to understand where the evidence agrees, where it conflicts, and what we still need to know.

Start with the business

Separate the retail engine from the cloud business. Look at the capital each requires, the durability of customer demand, and how much cash remains after reinvestment.
